Privacy

Updated 15 November 2026

This planner keeps your coursework, schedule and daily checklist in order. This page says what it stores, what it reads, and who else sees any of it.

What is stored

Everything you put in the planner: work, due dates, courses, events, the checklist, settings, and your conversations with Abood, the planner's assistant. It is stored in a database run by Supabase. Every row belongs to your account and the database refuses to show it to anyone else.

Gmail

Connecting Gmail is optional and off until you turn it on in Settings. It asks Google for read-only access: the planner can search and read your email, and cannot send, delete, label or change anything.

  • Your email is only read when you ask Abood something that needs it, such as finding a booking confirmation. Nothing is read in the background.
  • Up to five matching emails are read for each question. Their text is passed to the AI model that writes Abood's answer, and is not stored. Abood's answer is kept in your conversation like any other reply.
  • Nothing Abood reads in an email can change your planner on its own. Any change he suggests after reading email waits for your yes.
  • The access Google grants is kept on the server and never sent to your browser or to any other app connected to the planner.
  • Disconnecting in Settings cancels the access at Google and deletes it here.

The planner's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to answer your own questions, is never used for advertising, is never sold, and is never used to train AI models.

AI models

Abood's answers are written by an AI model: Anthropic's Claude, or Google Gemini or Groq when Claude is unavailable. The model is sent the part of your planner the question needs, and email text when you asked about email. These services process it to write the answer.

Who else sees your data

No one. It is not sold, shared for advertising, or shown to other accounts. The only services that handle it are the ones named on this page, and only to run the planner.

Deleting it

In Settings, under Your data, you can export everything or delete your account. Deleting the account deletes all of its data, including any Gmail connection.
